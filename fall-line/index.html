Hold the phone facing you and twist it like a steering wheel. In the air, swipe up or down to flip, draw circles to spin, swipe sideways for a shifty, or hold for a grab. Swipe diagonally up-left or up-right for a cork 360. Down-left spreads the skis into an eagle. Down-right crosses them.
