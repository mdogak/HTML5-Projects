How to play

01   Line it upMove your face beneath a falling marble. Keep your face in view.

02   Take a biteOpen your mouth as the marble reaches your lips. Each catch earns 10 points.

03   Charge upEvery half second closed banks 1 extra point for your next catch, up to +5. Each marble that falls offscreen costs 2 points.
